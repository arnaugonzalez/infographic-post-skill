# editorial
White or warm paper (#ffffff / #faf7f2) with near-black ink (#0b1f3a or #1a1a1a) and one ink-blue or
oxblood accent. Display serif with real contrast (Fraunces, Newsreader, Source Serif 4) at weight
300 with one word in 600; a plain sans (IBM Plex Sans, Source Sans 3) for body. Small uppercase
kicker with wide tracking above the headline. Numbered rows separated by hairline rules; big
light-weight serif numerals. Figures set huge in the serif with the key number in the accent.
An italic serif pull-quote in the footer. Reads like a magazine page.

# blueprint
Pale blue-grey paper (#eef2f5) or deep blueprint blue (#12355b) with a faint 24px grid drawn as
1px lines. Monospace labels (JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono) in uppercase; a condensed grotesque
(Barlow Condensed, Archivo Narrow) for the headline. Components drawn as outlined rectangles with
square corners, dimension-style annotations and leader lines. One signal color (safety orange
#e4572e or yellow #f2c14e) only on the hero path. Reads like an engineering drawing.

# terminal
Off-black (#0e0f0c) or paper-white background, everything in one monospace family (JetBrains
Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono) at several sizes. Content shown as a command, its output and a
diff or log; the hero is the line that matters, highlighted with a single phosphor green
(#7ee787) or amber (#ffb000). Box-drawing characters or 1px rules, no rounded corners. Reads like
a screenshot of a real session, but typeset.

# swiss
White, black and one strong primary (red #e30613 or blue #0047bb). A neo-grotesque used with
intent (Archivo, Inter Tight at 800 with tight tracking, or Familjen Grotesk) — never default
weights. Strict 12-column grid, flush-left ragged-right text, a huge number or word bleeding off
the edge, generous negative space. Thick rules (4-8px) as structure. Reads like a poster.

# brutalist
Raw: white or concrete grey (#d9d9d6), black 2-3px borders, square corners, no shadows. Heavy
grotesque (Archivo Black, Space Grotesk 700) mixed with monospace data. Elements deliberately
misaligned or overlapping by a few pixels; one loud flat color block (#ffde21 or #ff4d00) behind
the hero. Labels like stickers. Reads like a zine.
